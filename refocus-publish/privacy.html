Voice Refocus Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 3, 2026

Voice Refocus is a browser extension that reduces distracting background noise so speech is easier to follow.

Data we process

When you press Start, Voice Refocus captures audio from that browser tab and processes it locally on your device. Audio is not uploaded to our servers.

Data we store

We store your Focus strength preference in Chrome’s extension storage on your device.

Data we do not collect

  • No account required
  • No analytics SDKs in the extension
  • No sale of personal data

Permissions

Tab audio capture and related permissions are used only to provide the speech-clarity feature you enable.
