Name: Voice Refocus - Remove Distracting Background Noise
Short description (132 chars max):
Hear speech clearly on any tab. Removes distracting background noise and music — privately, on your device.

Detailed description:
Voice Refocus helps you focus on what’s being said in videos and streams.

• Remove distracting background noise and music
• Keep dialogue and speech easier to follow
• Works on YouTube and other sites with HTML5 video/audio
• Processing stays on your device — audio is not uploaded

How to use:
1. Open a video and start playing
2. Click the Voice Refocus icon
3. Press “Start on this tab”
4. Adjust Focus strength to taste

Category: Productivity
Language: English

Permission justifications (for store form):
• tabCapture — capture the current tab’s audio so Voice Refocus can reduce background noise
• offscreen — keep audio processing running reliably while you browse
• storage — remember your Focus strength setting
• activeTab — start enhancement for the tab you’re viewing

Single purpose:
Enhance tab audio to make speech clearer by reducing distracting background noise.
