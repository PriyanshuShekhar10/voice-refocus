How it works

One click on the tab you’re watching. No account. No upload.

01

Play a video

Open YouTube or any page with HTML5 video/audio and start playback.

02

Start Voice Refocus

Click the extension icon, then “Start on this tab.”

03

Tune focus

Raise Focus strength if noise still bleeds through; lower it if speech feels thin.

What you get

Clearer speech when background beds and scores get in the way.

Speech first

Built for talks, explainers, interviews, and lectures where music is too stimulating.

On-device

Audio stays on your machine. Preferences are saved locally in Chrome.

Simple controls

Start / stop on the current tab, plus one Focus strength slider. That’s it.

Privacy, plainly

When you press Start, tab audio is processed locally. We don’t require an account and we don’t upload your audio.
